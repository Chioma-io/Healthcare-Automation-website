Scheduling & Reminders

Fewer no-shows, a calendar that fills itself

CareFlow syncs with your booking system to send automated SMS and email reminders, manage waitlists, and re-book cancellations in real time — without a single phone call from your staff.

  • Two-way SMS and email reminders with confirm/cancel links
  • Automatic waitlist fill when a slot opens up
  • No-show prediction flags high-risk appointments early
Billing & Claims

Cleaner claims, faster reimbursement

Automated eligibility verification, coding checks, and claim scrubbing catch errors before submission — denials drop, and your billing team only touches the exceptions.

  • Real-time insurance eligibility checks at booking
  • Automated claim scrubbing against payer rules
  • Denial routing with suggested corrections
Compliance & Reporting

Audit-ready, every single day

Every automated action is logged with a timestamp and actor. Generate HIPAA, MIPS, and payer-required reports on a schedule — no more frantic exports before an audit.

  • Immutable audit trail across every automated workflow
  • Scheduled regulatory and quality reporting
  • Consent and disclosure tracking built in

Patient Intake

Paperwork that fills itself in

Patients complete intake, consent, and insurance forms on any device before they arrive. Data flows straight into your EHR, pre-populated and ready for the visit.

  • Mobile-friendly digital intake & e-signature
  • Insurance card capture with OCR auto-fill
  • Direct sync to your EHR — zero re-keying
Patient Communication

Stay top-of-mind, without the manual outreach

Automated recall campaigns bring patients back for preventive care, while secure two-way messaging lets your team answer questions without playing phone tag.

  • Recall campaigns triggered by care gaps
  • Secure, HIPAA-compliant two-way messaging
  • Automated post-visit satisfaction surveys
