We believe healthcare staff should spend time on patients, not paperwork

CareFlow was founded by a team of clinicians, engineers, and former hospital administrators tired of watching good people burn out on repetitive admin work.

Our Mission

Automation that respects clinical judgment

We started CareFlow after watching a close friend — a nurse practitioner — spend more hours on scheduling and billing follow-up than on actual patient care. That shouldn't be the trade-off.

Our mission is simple: automate the repetitive, rules-based work that eats up a care team's day, while keeping every clinical and judgment call firmly in human hands. We build tools that staff trust because they can see exactly what's happening and step in whenever they need to.

What We Stand For

The values behind every feature we ship

Privacy first

Every workflow is built HIPAA-compliant from day one, not bolted on after the fact.

Time back for staff

We measure success in hours returned to clinical and front-desk teams, not just tasks automated.

Transparent by default

Every automated action is logged and explainable — no black boxes making decisions silently.

Built with clinicians

Every feature is co-designed with the front-desk staff, nurses, and billers who use it daily.

Our Story

From one overworked clinic to 240+ care teams

2019

Founded in a spare clinic office

CareFlow started as a scheduling script built for a single family practice — automated reminders cut their no-show rate by a quarter in the first month.

2021

Billing & claims automation launches

After requests from early customers, we expanded into eligibility checks and claim scrubbing — and signed our first health system.

2023

SOC 2 Type II & 40+ EHR integrations

CareFlow completed SOC 2 Type II certification and built out pre-validated connectors for the most widely used EHR platforms.

2026

240+ clinics and health systems

Today CareFlow automates scheduling, billing, intake, and compliance reporting for care teams across the country.
